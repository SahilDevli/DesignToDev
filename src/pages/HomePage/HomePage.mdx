# Home Page

The Blue Sea Global landing page: navigation over a full-bleed poster, a band of four product cards,
three customer reviews and the footer. Use it as the site's entry route.

<Canvas of={HomePageStories.Desktop} />

<Controls />

## Sections

1. **Navigation Bar** — reused `NavigationBar` (contains the Login `Button`), overlaid on the poster with a 39% black fill.
2. **Landing poster** — bespoke: background photo and the `h1` "Everything For Your Space".
3. **Explore Products** — bespoke section and grey band; the four cards are reused `ProductCard`s.
4. **Product's Review** — bespoke: three `figure`s with photo, quote and author, alternating sides.
5. **Footer** — reused `Footer` (contains the Email `Input` and the Contact Now `Button`).

## Usage

```tsx
import HomePage from '../pages/HomePage/HomePage';

export function App() {
  return (
    <HomePage
      onLogin={() => openLogin()}
      onAddToCart={(product) => cart.add(product)}
      onSubscribe={(email) => subscribe(email)}
    />
  );
}
```

## Responsive

- **≥ 1920px** (`Large`) — bands full-bleed, content capped at 1440px and centred.
- **1440px+** (`Desktop`) — the Figma frame.
- **1024–1439px** (`Laptop`) — type, poster and spacing scale fluidly; cards 4-up, 2×2 below 1200px; reviews stay side by side.
- **768–1023px** (`Tablet`) — cards 2×2; each review stacks its photo above the quote.
- **< 768px** (`Mobile`) — cards 1-up; reviews stacked.

## Accessibility

- Landmarks: `header` (from `NavigationBar`, with `nav` "Primary"), `main`, `footer` (from `Footer`).
- One `h1` (poster title); `h2` for "Explore Products" and "Product's Review"; card titles are `h3`.
- Each section is a `region` named by its heading via `aria-labelledby`.
- Cards and reviews are `ul` lists; reviews use `figure` / `blockquote` / `figcaption`.
- Review photos have descriptive alt text; the poster photo is decorative (`alt=""`).
- All buttons, links, the rating stars and the email field are native, keyboard-operable controls provided by the reused components.

## Assumptions

- Content is capped at 1440px and centred; bands stay full-bleed (21px gutters at 1482px).
- The nav stays overlaid on the poster; below 1024px it wraps taller, so the poster title gets 176px top padding and the poster grows to fit.
- Cards go 4-up → 2×2 (768–1199px) → 1-up (< 768px); 3-up was skipped because it truncates titles and orphans the fourth card.
- Below 1440px the per-row review offsets become one fluid padding; below 1024px reviews stack with the photo first at 3:2.
- `#f2f2f2` and `#d9d9d9` have no design token and are kept as SCSS variables.
- Serif line height is 1.3em, from the Figma line heights.
